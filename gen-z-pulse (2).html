OUR STORY

Built by Gen Z. Powered by real voices.

Gen Z Pulse is a student-led market research initiative founded in Palo Alto, California, with a simple belief: the best way to understand the next generation is to hear directly from them.

We're bringing together students who share their authentic perspectives on brands, products, technology, food, health, beauty, and the trends shaping everyday life.

At the same time, we create a valuable connection with local businesses—from restaurants and retailers to consumer brands and community organizations. By listening directly to young consumers, businesses can better understand their preferences, test new ideas, and make more informed decisions.

Through original surveys, custom research, and published insights, Gen Z Pulse gives students a stronger voice while helping businesses understand the generation shaping what's next.
